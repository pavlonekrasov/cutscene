- **Captions build a word every 2–3 frames, the next word waiting in grey.** 'v0 now' → 'runs' → 'on your ChatGPT plan' (10.18 s) and 'Use any' → 'OpenAI' → 'model' (16.77 s): each word is on screen at low contrast for about 50 ms before it brightens.

Black rests of 60–100 ms separate every beat: the film never shows two things changing at once.
- **UI is small and stays where it would be in the app.** The account chip sits at the bottom left, the composer and model picker at the left third, the send button at the bottom right: about 15–25% of the frame each. The film keeps the app's layout and hides the rest, instead of centring and enlarging each piece.

**Eye at the cuts.** Caption (centred at the top third) → chip (bottom left, about 12% across): 38% sideways and 40% down, across a black rest. Caption → picker (left third): about 30%. ## 2. Classify

Hand-edited feature film in v0's minimal house style: locked frames, small centred panels on black, black rests. Conventions: question card, OAuth flow, a toggle, a model picker, a URL card, a partner lockup.

## 3. Diagnose

- Empty black frames of 0.4–1.1 s sit between beats. → Rests: a pause in the rhythm separates ideas the way a dissolve would, without blending them.
- Panels are a third of the frame wide, centred on black. → Isolation by negative space instead of zoom; it trades size for calm.
- Three windows of the sign-in flow in three shots. → Compression: one shot per step makes a multi-window OAuth flow read as one action.
- No camera movement at all. → A locked-frame genre: every change is a cut, so nothing competes with the UI's own motion.

## 4. Prescribe

- _new_: A timed black rest (0.4–1.0 s) between beats instead of a transition. (cut kind (Part 2))
- _confirms_: Cut-not-move: hand-edited reels often lock the frame. (camera off per scene (exists: act.wide))

## 5. Critique

- **I like:** The black rests make each panel arrive as a separate idea.
- **I wish:** At a third of the frame width the panels' text is about 9 px at feed size; negative space costs legibility.
- **I wonder:** Could the cut between two reel scenes be a black rest of a set length rather than a dissolve?
