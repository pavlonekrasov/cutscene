## 5. Critique

- **I like:** It films an agent the only honest way: you see what it does to a real app.
- **I wish:** The agent's log on the left is too small to read; it's texture, not information.
- **I wonder:** cutscene could record two pages in one scene and frame them side by side the way this frames agent and Simulator.

## Not for us

- The saturated orange gradient: brand colour, not a neutral backdrop.
