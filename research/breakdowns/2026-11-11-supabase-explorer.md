## 2. Classify

Hand-edited feature film for a developer workspace. Conventions: a wireframe-to-real opening, caption column beside cropped panels, long reading holds, an end card with a menu path.

## 3. Diagnose

- The dashboard starts as a wireframe and fills with real content. → Progressive reveal from structure to content: the new workspace is built in front of the viewer.
- The caption column and tilted crop repeat from the tokens film. → A house style: the same layout across launches makes the brand's films recognisable before the logo.
- Holds of 9 s and 13 s on SQL and results. → Reading time for code, at the cost of pace: shots lengthen through the film.
- The end card gives the menu path to switch the feature on. → Wayfinding as the call to action: the last thing said is where to go.

## 4. Prescribe

- _confirms_: Caption column beside the crop (Supabase ×2). (renderer caption layout (not built))
- _new_: End card with the feature's menu path when it is opt-in. (cards (not built))
- _adjusts_: Keep reading holds under ~4 s; split long results into two shots. (shot length (Part 2))

## 5. Critique

- **I like:** The wireframe filling with the real chart makes 'a new workspace' a reveal instead of a screenshot.
- **I wish:** The 9 s and 13 s holds double the film's length; the second half drags.
- **I wonder:** Could a reel's end card take the feature's menu path from the scene's own navigation steps?

## Not for us

- A drawn wireframe version of the dashboard.
- A terminal outside the app.
