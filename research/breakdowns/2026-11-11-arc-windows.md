## 2. Classify

Motion-designed launch montage: archival Windows footage, kinetic type, a counter, then real Arc UI on Windows. Conventions: nostalgia cold open, stat card, typing close-up, a gag before the end card.

## 3. Diagnose

- The search field is filmed so one word fills the width. → Legibility at phone size: typing is the action, so the text being typed is the largest thing in frame.
- The counter is a number ticker, not a sentence. → A stat card proves demand faster than a claim card.
- The camera never moves; the edit and the content carry all motion. → One motion source at a time: with fast cutting, a moving camera would add a second, competing motion.

## 4. Prescribe

- _confirms_: Typing is shown in close-up, the field at ×2 or more. (camera: typing zoom)
- _confirms_: Reframe by cutting in a fast montage; camera locked. (CAMERA.cutOver)
- _new_: A text card that builds word by word across cuts. (cards)

## 5. Critique

- **I like:** The history-of-Start cold open makes 'Finally' land as a punchline rather than a feature name.
- **I wish:** Thirteen seconds of UI at 0.9 s a shot never lets one flow finish; it sells the platform, not a task.
- **I wonder:** A counter card fed by a real number (scenes recorded, users seeded) could be a cutscene card type.

## Not for us

- Archival footage and the competitor gag (Edge in the bin).
