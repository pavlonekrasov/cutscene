## 2. Classify

Motion-designed launch film: the UI rebuilt as isolated, floating components on an illustrated backdrop. Conventions: a title sequence, a logo reveal, typed prompts, one wide shot of the real window at the end.

## 3. Diagnose

- The composer floats alone, at three times its size. → Isolation: removing the window strips every competing element, so the input is the only thing to read.
- The window is withheld until the last shot. → Delayed reveal: the viewer meets the behaviour before the product, then the window answers 'where is this'.
- Dissolves of 0.1–0.5 s between prompts. → Crossfades read as the same place, different moment: time passes, the location doesn't.

## 4. Prescribe

- _new_: Isolate the component: render one element's box alone on the backdrop at large scale. (camera / renderer (not built))
- _confirms_: Typing is the shot; the field is large. (camera: typing zoom)
- _new_: Dissolves, not cuts, between beats that happen in the same place. (transitions)

## 5. Critique

- **I like:** The prompts are the script: you learn what Dia does from what's typed, with no claim cards at all.
- **I wish:** Nothing shows a real page being read; the tab chips are the only link to the browser.
- **I wonder:** Could cutscene screenshot a locator's box from the real app and float it on the backdrop, so isolation stays the real UI?

## Not for us

- The illustrated sunburst is Dia's brand art.
