## 2. Classify

Teaser for a major version, built from isolated UI fragments on a material backdrop. Conventions: macro crops, a typed query, a state change shown in a burst, understated mono captions.

## 3. Diagnose

- Every UI moment is a fragment at ×3–5, alone on satin. → Isolation by default: no window chrome, so each fragment is the whole frame's subject.
- Four 0.4–0.9 s shots follow the emoji selection ring as its colour changes. → A micro-interaction as hero: a burst of cuts on one control's states makes a 2 px detail the memorable moment.
- The end card is small mono text, held 7.6 s. → Restraint as register: for a teaser, an understated caption reads as confidence.
- The satin is darker than the UI's own dark surfaces. → Figure-ground: dark panels on a darker field lose their edges.

## 4. Prescribe

- _new_: A state burst: three to five short focus shots on one control as it changes state. (burst cut on one locator (Part 2))
- _confirms_: Isolate the component on a backdrop. (camera / renderer (not built))
- _confirms_: Typing close-up at ×3–5 for a search query. (act.type framing (exists))

## 5. Critique

- **I like:** The four short shots of the selection ring changing colour turn a tiny detail into the film's hook.
- **I wish:** The black satin is darker than the panels, so their edges disappear and the fragments look cut out rather than floating.
- **I wonder:** Could a reel cut a 'state burst' automatically from the frames where one locator's style changes?
