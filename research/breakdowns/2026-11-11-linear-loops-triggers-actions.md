**Why it's here.** Rhythm: a dial of trigger names cuts on musical accents, slow at first (1.6–1.9 s a label), then quicker (0.4–0.9 s), then it settles into the wordmark; the clearest sound-led edit in the set.

**What the instrument can read.** A rotating dial with depth of field and blur: camera numbers are not usable. Cut times are.

## Frame-level beats

- Shot lengths 0.97 · 1.86 · 1.64 · 1.76, then 0.94 · 0.43 · 0.60 · 0.38: the cutting doubles its pace in the middle third, then relaxes into the 4.5 s slow push before the cards.

## Why it works

- Cuts sit on the music's accents rather than a metronome grid, so the edit feels played rather than snapped.

## What it teaches

- _new_: Cuts land on musical accents (onsets), not a beat grid; the evidence is weak (58% against 38% by chance, one film). (research-gated: beat snap)
- _confirms_: Cards enter through a blur (the wordmark resolves out of a blur). (CARD.enter (4 px blur))

- The abstract dial: brand motion design with no product UI in it.
