## 2. Classify

Abstract motion-design teaser with no product UI, cut to music. Conventions: a rotating dial of labels, an accelerating cut rhythm, a wordmark resolving out of blur.

## 3. Diagnose

- Labels hold 1.6–1.9 s, then 0.4–0.9 s, then the wordmark. → Accelerating rhythm into a resolution: the last long hold lands as the payoff.
- 11 of 19 cuts fall within 40 ms of a musical onset (chance about 38%). → Cutting on accents, not on a grid: the edit follows the music's phrasing.

## 4. Prescribe

- _new_: Cuts land on musical accents (onsets), not a beat grid; the evidence is weak (58% against 38% by chance, one film). (research-gated: beat snap)
- _confirms_: Cards enter through a blur (the wordmark resolves out of a blur). (CARD.enter (4 px blur))

## 5. Critique

- **I like:** The cuts quicken with the music, so the wordmark arrives at the end of an acceleration: shots of 1.6–1.9 s, then 0.4–0.9 s, then one 4.5 s push.
- **I wish:** There is no product in it; it names the feature but shows nothing of it.
- **I wonder:** Could a reel with a licensed track place its cuts on the track's onsets within ±40 ms?

## Not for us

- The abstract dial: brand motion design with no product UI in it.
