## 2. Classify

Motion-designed micro-launch for a single setting. Conventions: the control as title, a short path through settings, the result on devices, a word card and the logo.

## 3. Diagnose

- The opening toggle flips and the backdrop goes dark with it. → A continuity transition driven by the feature: the scene change is the setting itself, so the claim and the cut are one gesture.
- The Appearance row is lifted out of the list with a 'NEW!' badge. → Isolation plus a signpost: one row on a plain field tells the viewer where the feature lives.
- The selection ring travels System → Dark across two shots. → State change shown in the native control at ×3, the smallest frame that keeps the three options legible.
- Dissolves of 0.5–1.7 s on 10 of 14 cuts, still 86%. → A long crossfade reads as a mood shift (light into dark) but it is also about half the runtime spent in blends.

## 4. Prescribe

- _new_: When the feature changes the whole surface (theme, layout, density), hold the frame wide enough that the change fills it. (act.wide before the action (exists))
- _confirms_: Isolate the component: one settings row on a plain field. (camera / renderer (not built))
- _confirms_: Open on the navigation to the feature (settings → row → option). (reel script (skill))

## 5. Critique

- **I like:** The toggle that becomes the title makes the claim and the transition the same 2 s gesture.
- **I wish:** The dissolves average about 0.9 s on 1.2 s shots, so most shots are half-blended and none sit still long.
- **I wonder:** cutscene already records every scene in light and dark: could a reel cross-dissolve between the two takes of one scene to show a theme change?

## Not for us

- Drawn phone and star illustrations.
- Rebuilt settings rows.
