## 2. Classify

Montage teaser for an integration story. Conventions: an anchored element across cuts, accelerating cuts, an additive text build, the icon as logo.

## 3. Diagnose

- Notion's icon keeps its screen position across 20 cuts while everything around it changes. → The invariant across a montage is the subject: a match cut on position turns 'works with everything' into a picture.
- Cut interval drops from ~0.8 s to ~0.35 s, then one 1.3 s hold. → Accelerating rhythm builds momentum; the first long hold after it lands as the payoff.
- Cards append a phrase per beat. → Additive build: each phrase is read alone before the next joins it.
- Backgrounds are arbitrary art and photos. → Novelty per cut keeps attention, but carries no meaning about the product.

## 4. Prescribe

- _new_: Align consecutive shots on one shared element so every cut is a match cut on its position. (match cut on a locator (Part 2))
- _confirms_: Accelerate the cut rhythm into the payoff. (rhythm (measure: accelerates))
- _new_: Additive card build: append one phrase per beat. (cards (not built))

## 5. Critique

- **I like:** Holding the icon still while 20 docks change around it says 'works with everything' with no UI at all.
- **I wish:** The backgrounds bring novelty but no meaning; app screens behind the dock would carry the same rhythm and say more.
- **I wonder:** Could cutscene align consecutive scenes on one locator's box, so the cut between two scenes becomes a match cut?
