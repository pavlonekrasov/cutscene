## 2. Classify

Motion-designed phone film with real screens on device frames. Conventions: word-by-word text builds beside the device, edge-on rotation as a transition, a three-up comparison, an icon logo.

## 3. Diagnose

- Text builds word by word next to a still device. → Stagger as pacing: the build sets reading speed; the device waits.
- The phone turns edge-on to change state. → A continuity transition on the object itself: the same phone, a new screen, no cut.
- Three phones side by side. → Comparison by juxtaposition: three cases in one frame instead of three shots.

## 4. Prescribe

- _new_: Word-by-word text builds beside a held device frame. (cards (not built))
- _new_: Three device frames side by side to compare cases. (parallel action (Part 2))

## 5. Critique

- **I like:** Every transition is the phone moving, so the viewer never loses the object.
- **I wish:** The screens are small in the three-up; the difference between them is read from the icons, not the UI.
- **I wonder:** cutscene's phone format could take a 'turn' transition between two scenes of the same app.

## Not for us

- Device rotation in 3D is a render of a mock, not a recording.
